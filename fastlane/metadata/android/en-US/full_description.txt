vdpi is a zero-hassle, self-healing anti-DPI engine written in Rust that runs 100% locally on your Android device via Android's VpnService - with zero remote VPN servers, zero third-party relays, and zero telemetry.

Features:
- 7-stage adaptive TLS ClientHello desynchronization with persistent per-host caching
- Anti-poisoning encrypted DNS-over-HTTPS (DoH) with Cloudflare, Quad9, AdGuard, Google, and custom URL presets
- UDP/443 QUIC blocking and zero-lag UDP passthrough for voice and games
- Per-app split tunneling (Include or Exclude specific installed apps)
- Built-in connection diagnostics and live throughput telemetry
- Full English and Turkish localization
